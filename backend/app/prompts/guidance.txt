TASK
A blind person is pointing a phone camera at a {target} to photograph it. Judge this frame.
- `ready`: true only if the whole {target} is inside the frame, roughly flat, in focus and bright enough to read.
- `move`: if not ready, the ONE adjustment that helps most:
  left / right / up / down = which way to move the phone so the {target} becomes centred,
  closer = the {target} is too small, back = edges are cut off on several sides,
  hold = blurry from movement, light = the frame is very dark or black (always use light then),
  none = the frame is bright enough but no {target} is visible at all.
  If ready, use "hold".
