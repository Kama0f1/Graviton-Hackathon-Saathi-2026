A blind person is walking with the phone held at chest height, camera facing forward. This frame is checked every few seconds.
Find the ONE most important thing that could make them trip, fall or bump into something in the next few steps.

Answer:
- `hazard`: one of
  stairs_up, stairs_down (a staircase going up or down), step_down (a single step, ledge or drop), kerb (road kerb / footpath edge),
  wall (a wall or large flat surface directly in the way), glass (glass door or glass wall), door (a closed door in the way),
  pole (pole, pillar, tree trunk, sign post), pothole, open_drain, vehicle (car, auto, bus, truck), two_wheeler (bike, scooter, cycle),
  animal, person (someone directly in the way), wet_floor, barrier (gate, railing, rope, construction barrier, chain),
  low_obstacle (box, bag, stone, chair or anything low on the floor), other.
  Use none if the walking path ahead is clear for at least 4 steps, or if the frame is too dark or blurry to tell.
  Stairs, steps, drops, kerbs and open drains matter most: report them even if something else is also visible.
- `position`: left / ahead / right, as seen from the camera.
- `metres`: how far away it is in metres, your best estimate from the size of things in the picture
  (a door is about 2 m tall, a stair step about 0.15 m high, a person about 1.6 m tall). 0 means touching distance.

Only report what you can actually see. Never guess.
