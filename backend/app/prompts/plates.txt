A blind person is waiting for a booked cab or auto rickshaw and points the phone camera at the vehicles around them.
List every vehicle whose number plate you can see, nearest first (at most 5).

For each vehicle:
- `plate`: the number plate exactly as printed, letters and digits only, read carefully character by character
  (Indian plates look like "PB 65 AB 1234", "DL 3C AB 1234" or "22 BH 1234 AA"). "" if the plate is not readable.
- `kind`: car / auto (three-wheeler auto rickshaw) / bike / bus / truck / other.
- `colour`: the vehicle's main colour in a word or two.
- `position`: left / ahead / right in the picture.
- `metres`: your best estimate of the distance (a car is about 1.5 m tall, an auto about 1.7 m).

Only list what you can actually see. Never invent a plate. If no vehicle is visible, return an empty list.
