Saathi: UPI QR test sheet

TEST ONLY. These QR codes point to made-up UPI IDs (@saathitest) that do not exist. Do not try to pay with them in a real UPI app.
  1. Open Saathi on the phone → Read → Document → scan or upload the test bill below (Sharma Kirana Store, total ₹1,099).
  2. Go to Money → tap the QR button (right of Check change), or say “scan the QR”.
  3. Point the phone at a QR below, about 20–30 cm from the screen. Check what Saathi says against Expected.
  4. Repeat for each QR. For test 1 also try once without scanning the bill first: it should not say “matches”.
